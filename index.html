Copy, move or delete a PC Pokémon

You can copy from your party into an empty PC slot, but cannot move/delete a party Pokémon. Only empty destination slots are accepted. The party cannot be reordered, emptied or expanded in this version; doing so could interfere with the follower system.
Experimental build: validates Gen 3 record and flash-section checksums, but has not been tested loading an edited Pokémon state in Delta. Keep your original SVS and check your edited copy in-game before overwriting anything.
05 • Pokémon cleanup

Bad Egg scanner

Scans for flagged Gen 3 Bad Egg records in memory. Removal is optional and only clears positively flagged records; it does not change normal eggs.

Experimental: the same Pokémon may appear in multiple RAM copies. Keep a backup and test in Delta. Do not use this as a Pokémon storage editor.
06 • Advanced

Hex inspection & patching

For controlled follower-logic research or other known ThunderYellow offsets. Raw decompressed file offsets, not GBA ROM addresses.

Open hex editor
Manual patches modify only the specified offset; unlike the item editor, they do not synchronize copies. Always record the precise source of an offset.
