04 • Pokémon manager

Party & PC Pokémon Editor

Edit existing Pokémon directly in your .svs: moves, current PP, PP Ups, experience, friendship, held items, IVs, EVs and battle stats. PC Pokémon may be copied, moved and deleted. The special starter Pikachu is protected from copying or deletion.

📋 Paste Pokémon sets (Showdown / PKMDS format)

Paste one or several complete Pokémon sets, separated by blank lines. Preview the party or PC destination before importing. Level defaults to 100 when omitted; IVs default to 31, and EVs default to 0 unless specified.

Moves + Ability Only updates the Pokémon already in the selected slots. Their species must match the pasted sets. It preserves PID, OT, level, EXP, nature, shiny status, held item, IVs, EVs, happiness and follower identity. If Ability is omitted, it stays unchanged. New moves receive their default PP.
Import is blocked if it would replace our special follower Pikachu, cross the party's six-slot limit, or skip an earlier empty party slot. PC batches can continue into the next box.
Starter shortcut finds your original follower Pikachu even when not in slot 1. Paste multiple Pokémon sets, but only Pikachu’s moves and listed ability will be imported. “Shiny: Yes” and every other set property will be ignored by this shortcut.
⚡ Party Builder & Switcher

Build a lineup from the current six party Pokémon and your PC boxes. Save multiple named plans on this device, then activate one to exchange members between party and PC. The original follower Pikachu stays in his current party slot.

Switching modifies the working SVS only after confirmation. Party members not selected go into the PC slots vacated by incoming Pokémon. Save plans are stored in this browser, not in your ROM or original save file. Export the updated SVS to test in Delta.

Copy, move or delete a PC Pokémon

You can copy from your party into an empty PC slot, but cannot move/delete a party Pokémon. Only empty destination slots are accepted. The party cannot be reordered, emptied or expanded in this version; doing so could interfere with the follower system.
Experimental build: validates Gen 3 record and flash-section checksums, but has not been tested loading an edited Pokémon state in Delta. Keep your original SVS and check your edited copy in-game before overwriting anything.

06 • Pokémon cleanup

Bad Egg scanner

Scans for flagged Gen 3 Bad Egg records in memory. Removal is optional and only clears positively flagged records; it does not change normal eggs.

Experimental: the same Pokémon may appear in multiple RAM copies. Keep a backup and test in Delta. Do not use this as a Pokémon storage editor.
