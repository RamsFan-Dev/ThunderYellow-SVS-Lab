IPHONE & IPAD · IOS

Delta Game Emulator

Recommended for iPhone and iPad. Delta supports Game Boy Advance games and importing your own .gba files, plus regular saves, save states and controller support.

Get Delta — App Store ↗ Delta user guide ↗
  1. Install Delta on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Once ThunderYellow is publicly released, use our game installer and save the finished .gba in Files.
  3. In Delta, import your saved ThunderYellow game. Start a new save or restore a compatible regular in-game save.

Save states from different game builds are not necessarily compatible. Back up your saves first.

ANDROID · PHONES & TABLETS

Pizza Boy A Basic

Recommended for Android. Pizza Boy A Basic is a Game Boy Advance emulator available on Google Play. It doesn't include commercial games, so you'll use the ThunderYellow file you generate yourself.

Get Pizza Boy A — Google Play ↗ Alternative: RetroArch ↗
  1. Install Pizza Boy A Basic from Google Play (or RetroArch from its official website).
  2. Once the first release is live, use our game installer and save the completed .gba in your Downloads folder.
  3. In your Android emulator, browse to the downloaded .gba and open ThunderYellow.

RetroArch is more configurable and uses a separate GBA core. The official RetroArch website warns that its Google Play build is outdated.

The base game you will need

Where to get the right LeafGreen file

ThunderYellow's first public installer is designed for the exact original Pokémon LeafGreen (USA), Rev 1 (also called 1.1). A physical cartridge is one legitimate route to obtaining your own copy; a cartridge reader can create a personal game backup on a computer. We do not link to unauthorized ROM-download sites.

01 / SOURCE YOUR CARTRIDGE

Find an authentic LeafGreen cartridge

Explore secondhand listings for an original US Pokémon LeafGreen Game Boy Advance cartridge. Confirm authenticity, seller terms and cartridge condition before purchasing.

Browse cartridge listings ↗

These are third-party reseller listings, not Nintendo's store. Buying a cartridge does not guarantee it is the required Rev 1; verify your exact backup below.

02 / CREATE YOUR OWN BACKUP

Back up your cartridge to .gba

The GB Operator hardware and Epilogue Playback app can make personal backups of Game Boy Advance cartridges to your computer, including game data. Transfer the resulting .gba to your phone's Files or Downloads.

See GB Operator backup guide ↗

Requires compatible cartridge-reading hardware and a Windows, macOS or Linux computer. Rules about copying games vary by location; check local law.
