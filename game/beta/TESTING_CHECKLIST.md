# ThunderYellow Beta 1 — Tester checklist

**Status:** Release patch pending verification. This checklist is for testers once a verified patch becomes available.

## Getting started
- [ ] Supply your own compatible Pokémon LeafGreen USA Rev 1 / 1.1 backup where permitted by law.
- [ ] Verify the exact source with the site's local installer; never upload or share the original ROM.
- [ ] Keep the original and all existing saves untouched.
- [ ] Generate a separately named ThunderYellow game when the installer enables Beta 1.
- [ ] Start a new game to test the full introduction.

## Gameplay
- [ ] Intro and initial Pikachu follower startup.
- [ ] Follower after wild battles, trainer battles, and moves between maps.
- [ ] Pokémon Center entry/exit, tall grass, and ledge jumps.
- [ ] Surf entry, water travel, encounters while surfing, and shore exit.
- [ ] Basic HMs and badges needed for field progression.
- [ ] Party switching, captures, leveling, and healing.
- [ ] Progress through Gyms, story events, and available legendary encounters.
- [ ] Save in game, close the emulator, reopen, and continue.

## Reporting
Include the **public version**, phone/OS, emulator/version, location, steps to reproduce, expected/actual result, and screenshot or short video. Avoid uploading the original ROM or sensitive files.

Report bugs: https://github.com/RamsFan-Dev/ThunderYellow-SVS-Lab/issues/new?template=bug-report.yml
