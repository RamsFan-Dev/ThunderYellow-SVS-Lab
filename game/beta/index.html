What Beta 1 will test

Help verify real gameplay, not only whether a ROM starts. Exact availability and behavior will be confirmed using the eventual public patch.

01 / FOLLOWER

Pikachu companion

Walk, jump ledges, enter Pokémon Centers, fight battles, use Surf, and return to shore. Note any missing or misaligned follower behavior.

02 / BATTLE MENUS

Move info & Battle Coach

Test in-battle move descriptions and optional advice about Pokémon and attacks. Recommendations are estimates, not guaranteed best choices.

03 / PROGRESSION

Story, badges & saves

Play from a fresh save through gyms, travel and transitions. Back up your save before changing emulator settings or game builds.

04 / ENCOUNTERS

Pokémon & events

Report wild encounters, event Pokémon and Legendary encounters that behave unexpectedly. Some access paths still require wider verification.

Known testing risks

Pre-release software: Features may malfunction or freeze, saves may not transfer between builds, and battle recommendations can favor a risky switch. Some historical experimental builds showed title-screen graphics artifacts. The final beta candidate has not yet been verified against the public LeafGreen base. Preserve your original ROM and backed-up .sav/.svs files.
