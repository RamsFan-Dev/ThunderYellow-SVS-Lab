How beta testing will work

01 / SOURCE

Use your own game

Provide a lawfully obtained Pokémon LeafGreen (USA) Rev 1 / 1.1 ROM, where permitted by applicable law. The browser installer verifies its SHA-1 locally. The file never leaves your device.

02 / PATCH

Generate a new game

After the verified Beta 1 patch is published, the installer will create a separate ThunderYellow GBA file locally. Do not overwrite your original ROM or existing saves.

03 / PLAY

Test and report

Start a new game in a GBA emulator, keep save backups, and send a reproducible report when something fails. Use the community for discussion.

What we need testers to check

Follower & travel

  • Pikachu follows after battles and building transitions
  • Ledges, tall grass, and Pokémon Centers
  • Surf entry, long swims, and returning to shore
  • Fly, Strength, Flash, Cut, Rock Smash, and Waterfall progression

Playthrough & reliability

  • New-game introduction and key story events
  • Trainer and wild encounters, capture and party switching
  • Gym progression, legendary encounters, and post-game access
  • Saving, quitting, reopening, and continuing in the same build

Optional experimental battle-assistant features are still in internal testing and are not promised for Beta 1. Do not assume every legendary encounter or post-game path has been validated.

Report a problem

Please include the public version shown on the downloads page, your emulator and device, the location or battle, what you expected, what happened, and step-by-step instructions to reproduce it. A screenshot or short video is particularly helpful. Never post personal information or upload copyrighted original ROMs.

Known limitations

Beta software: Save states are not interchangeable guarantees across versions. Keep separate in-game save backups. Pikachu follower transitions and selected event routes are still being evaluated. If a game freezes, preserve a screenshot and the steps leading to it; do not overwrite your last working save.
