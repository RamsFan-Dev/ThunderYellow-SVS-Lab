An electric return to Kanto

Built for the GBA and tested in Delta on iOS. Development focuses on a Pikachu that follows the player, classic region exploration, and fixes confirmed through actual gameplay sessions.

01 / COMPANION

Follower Pikachu

Travel with Pikachu close behind. Recent preview testing has concentrated on staying synchronized across Surf, battles and shore transitions.

02 / DISCOVERY

Wild encounters and events

Expanded Gen III encounter testing includes different wild Pokémon, legendary encounters and event progression. Individual availability is still being verified.

Current project status

The public website tracks playable tests conservatively. Test 125N passed specified Surf-entry, shore-exit and subsequent battle checks, but it is still a preview and may have undiscovered issues.

Verified areas

Selected follower recovery, Surf transitions and battle follow-up behavior have been exercised in Delta. The v1 → Test 125N patch has also been checked byte-for-byte against the reference preview ROM.

Still being tested

Complete end-to-end playthrough, remaining legendary access paths, save-state compatibility across builds, and broad device compatibility are not certified.
