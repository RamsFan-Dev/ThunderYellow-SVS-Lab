An electric return to Kanto

Built for the GBA and tested in Delta on iOS. Development focuses on a Pikachu that follows the player, classic region exploration, and fixes confirmed through actual gameplay sessions.

01 / COMPANION

Follower Pikachu

Travel with Pikachu close behind. Recent preview testing has concentrated on staying synchronized across Surf, battles and shore transitions.

02 / DISCOVERY

Wild encounters and events

Expanded Gen III encounter testing includes different wild Pokémon, legendary encounters and event progression. Individual availability is still being verified.
