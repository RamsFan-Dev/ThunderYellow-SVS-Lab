Questions

Has ThunderYellow already been released?

Yes, Beta 1 is the first public testing release. It is not a final stable edition.

Can I download the game today?

Yes. Use the browser installer with your own verified LeafGreen USA Rev 1 ROM. The site creates the completed .gba locally and checks it before offering a download.

Where can I find the exact LeafGreen ROM?

We do not host or link to unauthorized ROM downloads. You can look for an authentic secondhand LeafGreen GBA cartridge, make your own .gba backup with a suitable reader such as the GB Operator, then confirm the exact USA Rev 1 fingerprint in our installer. Not every LeafGreen cartridge is the supported revision. See the device and sourcing guide.

How far along is development?

The Beta 1 patch and its output are verified. Gameplay, follower transitions, events and emulator compatibility still need more public testing.

Will the website distribute a complete .gba ROM?

The website generates a complete ThunderYellow .gba locally from your compatible LeafGreen original and the verified Beta 1 patch. It will not host or distribute Nintendo's original ROM.
